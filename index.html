OUR STORY

Same Counter. New Generation.

The Malt Shop has been a Main Street landmark in Ephraim since 1950 — the red-and-white checkers, the counter stools, and the roadside sign out front are all part of the family. In 2024, after decades behind the grill, our longtime owners retired and handed the spatula to their daughter and son-in-law, who are keeping every tradition that made this place special.

  • 1950The Malt Shop opens its doors on Main Street.
  • DecadesGenerations of Ephraim families and Snow College students grab a seat at the counter.
  • 2024The founding family passes the shop to the next generation.
  • TodayStill flipping burgers and blending malts — come say hi.
